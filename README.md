# Favorite Movie: Everything Everywhere All at Once
A 2022 comedy-drama film written and directed by Daniel Kwan and Daniel Scheinert. I love this movie because of the rollercoaster-feeling it gave me while watching it. Beneath all the chaos, the film delivers a beautiful message about the importance of being kind. It reminds us that even when life feels overwhelming and meaningless, choosing kindness, understanding, and compassion can make all the difference.
